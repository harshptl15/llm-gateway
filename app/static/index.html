How it works, in plain English

Every question goes through the same five steps, in a few milliseconds before the AI is ever involved.

1

Checks your key

Like a membership card: only apps with a valid key can use the gateway.

2

Prevents overuse

Each key has a per-minute limit and a daily budget, so one runaway app can't run up a huge bill.

3

Reads the meaning

The question is turned into a "meaning fingerprint" and compared with every question answered before.

4

Reuses or asks

If a past question is similar enough, its answer is reused. If not, the AI is asked and its answer is remembered.

5

Keeps a receipt

Every question is logged with what it cost and what it saved. That's where the numbers above come from.

Why not reuse even more answers?

Because questions that sound alike aren't always the same question. To the matcher, How do I become an introvert? and How do I become less of an introvert? look 95% alike, but they need opposite answers.

So there's a similarity bar, currently 95%. Lower it and more questions get free, instant answers, but more of those answers are for a slightly different question. We measured this on 4,000 real questions from Quora that people had labeled "same" or "different": with a 90% bar, somewhere between 1 in 6 and 1 in 3 reused answers were for a different question. At 95%, that drops to between 1 in 9 and 1 in 4. The full numbers are in the project README.

What the numbers mean

Answered from memory
The share of questions the gateway answered by reusing a past answer. Each one cost $0 and skipped the wait.
Similarity
How close in meaning a new question is to the closest one answered before, from 0% (unrelated) to 100% (identical).
Money saved
What those reused answers originally cost. The AI would have charged that again without the gateway.
Asked the AI
No past question was similar enough, so the gateway paid for a fresh answer and saved it for next time.
